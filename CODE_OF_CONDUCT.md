# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, accessible, and harassment-free community.
Participate with empathy, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy and
publisher ownership, and avoid inflammatory or discriminatory conduct.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior threatens the community, project integrity, or another person's safety.
Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact available on the
GitHub profile; do not publish personal details in an issue.
